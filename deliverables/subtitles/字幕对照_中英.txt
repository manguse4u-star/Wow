第 2 页 · 定义
  中: 扩张速率是洋底离开洋中脊的速度。
  EN: A spreading rate is how fast the seafloor moves away from a ridge.
  中: 单侧板块相对洋脊的速度叫半扩张速率，
  EN: One plate's speed relative to the ridge is the half-spreading rate;
  中: 两块板块分开的速度叫全扩张速率，一般每年 2 到 15 厘米。
  EN: the speed at which the two plates separate is the full rate, typically 2 to 15 cm per year.
