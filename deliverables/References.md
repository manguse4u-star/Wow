## Agency sources
1. Kious, W. J. & Tilling, R. I. (1996). *This Dynamic Earth: The Story of Plate Tectonics*. U.S. Geological Survey (general-interest publication). Online edition: https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/dynamic.html. See especially the sections "Developing the theory" (magnetic striping and seafloor spreading) and "Understanding plate motions".
2. NOAA Ocean Exploration. "What is a mid-ocean ridge?" Ocean Facts. https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/facts/mid-ocean-ridge.html

## Textbooks
3. Fowler, C. M. R. (2005). *The Solid Earth: An Introduction to Global Geophysics* (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press. (Ch. 3, plate tectonics and marine magnetic anomalies; Ch. 9, oceanic lithosphere and ridges.)
4. Kearey, P., Klepeis, K. A. & Vine, F. J. (2009). *Global Tectonics* (3rd ed.). Wiley-Blackwell. (Seafloor spreading, magnetic anomalies, ocean ridges.)
5. Lowrie, W. (2007). *Fundamentals of Geophysics* (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press. (Palaeomagnetism and marine magnetic anomalies.)

## Primary literature
6. Vine, F. J. & Matthews, D. H. (1963). Magnetic anomalies over oceanic ridges. *Nature*, 199, 947–949. https://doi.org/10.1038/199947a0. (Symmetric magnetic stripes as a record of seafloor spreading and field reversals.)
7. Parsons, B. & Sclater, J. G. (1977). An analysis of the variation of ocean floor bathymetry and heat flow with age. *Journal of Geophysical Research*, 82(5), 803–827. https://doi.org/10.1029/JB082i005p00803. (Depth ∝ √age, used for the cross-section shape.)
8. Müller, R. D., Sdrolias, M., Gaina, C. & Roest, W. R. (2008). Age, spreading rates, and spreading asymmetry of the world's ocean crust. *Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems*, 9, Q04006. https://doi.org/10.1029/2007GC001743. (Global half-spreading rates; spreading is not always symmetric.)
9. DeMets, C., Gordon, R. G. & Argus, D. F. (2010). Geologically current plate motions. *Geophysical Journal International*, 181(1), 1–80. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246X.2009.04491.x. (Spreading rates measured from magnetic anomalies; MORVEL.)
