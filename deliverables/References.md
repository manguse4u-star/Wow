# References — Spreading Rates (GEOL0012 video blog)

**Style:** Harvard (*Cite Them Right*), one of the styles UCL Library supports. Entries are in alphabetical order by first author, with no numbering. Book and journal titles are in italics, article titles in single quotation marks, and DOIs are given as `https://doi.org/` links.

**Where it appears:** at the **end** of the video, as a 4-second end card (`video/Spreading_Rates_References_EndCard.mp4`, also hidden slide 5 of the PPTX). Short in-text citations sit on slides 2–4.

## Reference list

Fowler, C.M.R. (2005) *The solid Earth: an introduction to global geophysics*. 2nd edn. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

Kious, W.J. and Tilling, R.I. (1996) *This dynamic Earth: the story of plate tectonics*. Reston, VA: U.S. Geological Survey. Available at: https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/dynamic.html (Accessed: 17 November 2026).

Müller, R.D. et al. (2008) 'Age, spreading rates, and spreading asymmetry of the world's ocean crust', *Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems*, 9(4), Q04006. Available at: https://doi.org/10.1029/2007GC001743.

NOAA Ocean Exploration (no date) *What is a mid-ocean ridge?* Available at: https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/facts/mid-ocean-ridge.html (Accessed: 17 November 2026).

Parsons, B. and Sclater, J.G. (1977) 'An analysis of the variation of ocean floor bathymetry and heat flow with age', *Journal of Geophysical Research*, 82(5), pp. 803–827. Available at: https://doi.org/10.1029/JB082i005p00803.

Vine, F.J. and Matthews, D.H. (1963) 'Magnetic anomalies over oceanic ridges', *Nature*, 199(4897), pp. 947–949. Available at: https://doi.org/10.1038/199947a0.
